# Intern at IMC Trading

## Intro
I started my internship with IMC Trading as a HW engineer on 25 May 2026. But the events leading up to this surreal week started over a year ago. I type this blog from the balcony of my apartment in Amsterdam-Zuid, very proud of how far I have come, and very hopeful of how much farther I can go.

Quite some time ago - much earlier than eCTF 2025 started, I came to be aware of this exciting position at IMC as a hardware engineer. Now believe me, I have always been very curious about how computers work under the hood. While a lot of people are generally interested in understanding how a computer executes programs meant for it, I have always been more interested in how the hardware makes everything tick. And as I built a better understanding of this, I became extremely interested in what you can do to the hardware to make a computer blazing fast. I also keep myself updated on new events in the commercial PC/CPU industry. You may easily find me looking at reviews of new laptops now and then.

So coming back to where it began - I came to know there is an interesting application of superfast computer hardware to make very high frequency profitable trades. It also represented a very unique opportunity - do the thing I love in an environment surrounded by the absolute best people out of college. I had my heart set on this role, and here I am!

## First week

My major motivation for this blog is not for others to read(although I would be more than happy if someone did), but for me to understand how I evolved as a person over the 10ish weeks for the internship. Technical improvement aside, I really value being a great person - one who can work with everyone, play with everyone and be there for everyone. And my first week at IMC highlighted the importance of it. The intern cohort I met, which was largely people I had never met before, some of whom I will probably not meet again soon, was the sweetest and most fun group ever. They were brilliant people, but fun to be with at the same time - the exact kind of people I want to be around.

After realising I have to leave to Amsterdam with just 5 days with these people, and the value of the time I spent with them, I decided to make this blog. I want to reflect on how my personal relationships evolve, how I develop as a person and an overall diary of events over the summer.

Keep a lookout for more!
